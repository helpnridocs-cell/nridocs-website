Overview

Guidance and assistance for embassy or consular attestation of Indian documents for overseas use. Our team helps clients understand the documentation requirements and the applicable process. Exact eligibility, authority, fees and timelines depend on the individual case.

Documents commonly required

  • Original document
  • MEA/state/other prior authentication where applicable
  • Passport/ID details
  • Destination country and purpose of use

How the process works

  1. Review destination-country requirements
  2. Check the required authentication sequence
  3. Assist with submission to the applicable authority
  4. Track the process where possible
  5. Guide on document return/delivery

Important note

Embassy requirements, fees and processing steps vary by country and document type.

FAQ

Frequently asked question

Who may need Embassy Attestation?

The requirement depends on the applicant's circumstances, destination and the authority requesting the document. Contact NRIDocs with your country of residence and purpose so the applicable process can be reviewed.

How long will it take?

Timelines vary according to the document, issuing authority, applicant circumstances and whether additional verification is required. An estimate can be discussed after reviewing the case.

Can I check the requirements before placing an order?

Yes. Contact NRIDocs first and share the service, country of residence and purpose of use. We can help identify the basic applicable requirements.
