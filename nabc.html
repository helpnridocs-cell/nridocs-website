Service overview

What we help with

Assistance for cases where the usual birth record is not available in the relevant government registry.

Exact requirements can change based on the issuing authority, state, applicant status and destination-country rules. We review the case before confirming the route.

Typical supporting documents

  • Passport / identity proof
  • School or hospital records where available
  • Parent documents where applicable
  • Affidavit or supporting declaration where required
Process

How this service works

01

02

Supporting evidence

We identify the documents and declarations relevant to the case.

03

Application coordination

The applicable authority process is coordinated.

04

NABC outcome

The non-availability certificate is obtained where the authority permits.
