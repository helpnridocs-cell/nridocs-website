FAQS

Frequently asked questions

Do you provide services for NRIs outside India?

Yes. NRIDocs focuses on documentation assistance for NRIs and international clients. Eligibility and process depend on the specific service and authority.

Can I check requirements before placing an order?

Yes. Contact us with your service, country of residence and purpose of use so the basic requirements can be reviewed.

How long does a service take?

Timelines vary by service, authority and applicant circumstances. We can discuss an estimate after reviewing the requirement.

Can you help with documents for use abroad?

Yes. We assist with applicable certificates, apostille, attestation and related documentation requirements for overseas use.
