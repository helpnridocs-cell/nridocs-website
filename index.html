Helpful answers

Questions NRIs usually ask.

Can I apply from outside India?

Many documentation services can be coordinated remotely, depending on the document, issuing authority and your specific circumstances. Contact NRIDocs for a case-specific route.

How do I know which service I need?

Use the Service Finder above or contact the team with your purpose, country and document details.

Do requirements vary by country?

Yes. The destination country, receiving authority and intended use can affect whether apostille, attestation, translation or additional steps are required.
