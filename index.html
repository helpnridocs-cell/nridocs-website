FAQS

Questions clients ask before starting

Can NRIDocs help if I live outside India?

Yes. NRIDocs is designed for NRI and international clients. Availability depends on the specific service and authority.

Can I check the requirements before paying?

Yes. You can submit an enquiry first. We can review the basic requirement and explain the applicable next steps.

Do you handle original documents?

Where a service requires physical originals, the applicable process and safe document handling will be explained before proceeding.

How quickly will I receive a response?

Response time depends on working hours and enquiry volume. WhatsApp is available for direct communication.

Are service fees and timelines fixed?

No. Fees and timelines can vary by document type, issuing authority, applicant circumstances and destination-country requirements.

Can you help with documents needed for another country?

Yes, where the applicable service is available. Share the destination country and purpose so the correct documentation route can be assessed.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us what document you need and where you live. We'll help you identify the likely route.
