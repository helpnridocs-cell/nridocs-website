FAQS

Frequently asked questions

Some common questions before starting your documentation request.

Do you provide services for NRIs living outside India?

Yes. NRIDocs focuses on documentation assistance for NRIs and international clients. Service eligibility and process depend on the specific document and authority involved.

Can I first check my requirements before placing an order?

Yes. Use the enquiry form or WhatsApp us with your requirement so the applicable documents and process can be reviewed.

How long does a service take?

Timelines vary by service, document type, authority and applicant circumstances. We can provide an estimated timeline after reviewing the requirement.

Can you help with documents required for use abroad?

Yes. NRIDocs assists with applicable apostille, attestation, certificate and related documentation requirements for overseas use.

How do I start?

Submit the enquiry form below or contact us on WhatsApp. Share your service requirement and country of residence, and our team can guide you on the next steps.
